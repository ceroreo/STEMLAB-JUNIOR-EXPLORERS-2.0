SULAM Community STEM Overview

This web application translates hands-on Service-Learning Malaysia – University for Society (SULAM) STEM outreach modules into tablet-friendly interactive simulations for primary school students:

  • 1. Eco-Sort Heroes (Science): Drag & drop community waste into standard Malaysian recycling & compost bins.
  • 2. River Water Filter Lab (Chemical/Civil Eng): Drag filtration media layers in order & slide water pressure to purify muddy river water.
  • 3. Solar Smart Microgrid (Electrical Eng): Slide the solar roof tilt angle to track the sun & drag components into a closed circuit.
  • 4. Bridge Truss Builder (Mechanical/Structural Eng): Drag triangular truss frames onto bridge joints & slide the load tester.
  • 5. Robo-Green Code Maze (Technology/Coding): Drag directional command blocks to program an eco-robot to plant trees.
  • 6. Gear & Pulley Balance Lab (Mathematics): Drag mechanical gears & slide counterweights to balance primary math ratios.
